# Pass by pointer - swap_ints from the Coding Exam 2 Preparation lab.
# Two stack frames: main and swap_ints. Arrows go from swap_ints into main.

TITLE: swap_ints: changing the caller's variables
TOPIC: passbyptr
TEXT: C copies every argument into the function. To let a function change your variables, pass their addresses.
TEXT: Inside the function, * follows each address back into main's frame.

CODE:
void swap_ints(int *a, int *b) {
    int temp = *a;
    *a = *b;
    *b = temp;
}

int main() {
    int x = 5, y = 10;
    swap_ints(&x, &y);
}
END

STEP: 8
EXPLAIN: main makes x and y.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 5 | changed
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 10 | changed

STEP: 1
EXPLAIN: Calling swap_ints makes a new frame. a and b are copies of the addresses &x and &y.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 5
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 10
MEM: swap_ints | a | int* | 1980 | ->x | changed
MEM: swap_ints | b | int* | 1988 | ->y | changed

STEP: 2
EXPLAIN: *a follows the arrow to x and copies 5 into temp.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 5
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 10
MEM: swap_ints | a | int* | 1980 | ->x
MEM: swap_ints | b | int* | 1988 | ->y
MEM: swap_ints | temp | int | 1996 | 5 | changed

STEP: 3
EXPLAIN: *a = *b copies y's value into x. This is a change to main's x, not to a copy.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 10 | changed
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 10
MEM: swap_ints | a | int* | 1980 | ->x
MEM: swap_ints | b | int* | 1988 | ->y
MEM: swap_ints | temp | int | 1996 | 5

STEP: 4
EXPLAIN: *b = temp puts the old x into y. The swap is done.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 10
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 5 | changed
MEM: swap_ints | a | int* | 1980 | ->x
MEM: swap_ints | b | int* | 1988 | ->y
MEM: swap_ints | temp | int | 1996 | 5

STEP: 9
EXPLAIN: swap_ints returns and its frame is gone. x and y stay swapped: x = 10, y = 5.
MEM: main | x | int | 2000 | 10
MEM: main | y | int | 2004 | 5
